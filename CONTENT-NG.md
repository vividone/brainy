# Content tracker — Nigerian (UBE)

Where the Nigerian pack actually stands, and how it got there.

- **Coverage below is generated.** Run `npm run content:track` after adding
  skills; everything between the coverage markers is rewritten from the pack
  itself, so the numbers cannot drift. Everything outside them is written by
  hand and is preserved.
- **Source of truth for what to build:** **Lagos State Unified Scheme of Work for Primary Schools, 2021 Edition** —
  the state's own curriculum document, following NERDC, published free as PDFs
  per subject for Primary 1–3 and 4–6. It gives topic, term and week for every
  class. Only the topic list and its sequence are taken from it; every question
  is generated by us.
  <br>The aggregator that hosts those PDFs states "the entirety of this site is
  protected by copyright, all rights reserved", so its lesson notes and exam
  questions are **not** used and none of them are in the product.
- **Two checks, not one.** `npm run smoke` proves every generator makes
  well-formed, answerable items. It cannot see that two options are the same
  number, or that an explanation contradicts its own answer. `npm run sample
  <skill-id>` prints real questions with their options and answers, and that is
  what catches those. New skills go through both.

## Status

**The lead pack, and the only one a family can rely on today.** Four subjects
authored across all six classes.

Known imbalance: Basic 3 has roughly four times the maths of its neighbours,
because it was built first, for one child. Basic 4 maths is now the thinnest at
seven skills — below Basic 2 — and is the next thing to fix.

Seven subjects are declared but unauthored. They show as locked rather than
empty, so nobody opens a subject with nothing in it.

## What is next

1. **Basic 4 maths**, to bring it level with its neighbours.
2. **Quantitative Reasoning tracking the maths topics term by term.** The
   scheme embeds a QR sample in *every* maths topic — factor-split boxes,
   machine diagrams, percentage-to-fraction matching. Ours does not follow it.
   This is the highest-value structural fix and it lifts all six classes.
3. **English Studies from the scheme.** The Primary 4–6 PDF is downloaded and
   still unread.
4. Still in the scheme and still missing at Basic 5–6: ratio and proportion,
   simple equations, plane figures and volume, division of decimals.
