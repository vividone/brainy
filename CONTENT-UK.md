# Content tracker — British (National Curriculum)

Where the UK pack actually stands, and how it got there.

- **Coverage below is generated.** Run `npm run content:track` after adding
  skills; everything between the coverage markers is rewritten from the pack
  itself, so the numbers cannot drift. Everything outside them is written by
  hand and is preserved.
- **Source of truth for what to build:** **The National Curriculum in England: mathematics programmes of study, Key
  Stages 1 and 2** (Department for Education). It is published free and states
  the statutory content year by year, which maps directly onto our year bands.
  <br>English uses the same document's *English programmes of study*, where
  spelling, punctuation and grammar are set out per year.
- **Two checks, not one.** `npm run smoke` proves every generator makes
  well-formed, answerable items. It cannot see that two options are the same
  number, or that an explanation contradicts its own answer. `npm run sample
  <skill-id>` prints real questions with their options and answers, and that is
  what catches those. New skills go through both.

## Status

**Early. Maths only, and not yet across the years.**

This pack began as proof that the engine switches curriculum cleanly rather
than as a product a British family could use, and it did that job. It is not
yet something to sell on.

Anything the marketing says about this pack must match the table below. A
parent who picks an empty year gets an empty app, and that is the fastest way
to lose the trust the rest of the product is built on.

## What is next

1. **Say what is true on the landing page** until this pack is real. That is
   copy, not content, and it should not wait.
2. **Year 4 first**, then Years 5 and 6 — the years where a parent is most
   likely to be looking for extra practice.
3. Years 1 and 2 after that. Early number needs different question shapes from
   the Nigerian pack, not a translation of them.
4. English (SPaG) once maths is continuous across the years.
